Unofficial Crafting Fix build of Item Scroller for **Minecraft 1.21.11** (Fabric).
Based on official Item Scroller 0.30.8 (sakura-ryoko `LTS/1.21.11`) + Andrews54757's itemscroller-crafting-fix.

**Requires:** Fabric Loader 0.19.5+ and **MaLiLib for 1.21.11 (>= 0.27.19, < 0.28.0)**. Remove the regular Item Scroller jar.

Changes from official 0.30.8:
- New `dropRecipeRemainder` (default ON): throws out recipe remainders (Glass Bottles from Honey Blocks, etc.) while mass crafting.
- `massCraftInterval` defaults to 1 tick.

日本語: 1.21.11 用の非公式 Crafting Fix 版です。1.21.11 用 MaLiLib が必要です。通常の Item Scroller とは入れ替えて使ってください。
